Loaded Dice: Delete Your Data

Loaded Dice is made by Jilly Bean Studios. Last updated: November 13, 2026

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Email support@jillybeanstudios.com from the email address you used to contact us.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my Loaded Dice data”. You don’t need to give a reason.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm when it’s done.

Loaded Dice has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

What we delete

The only information Jilly Bean Studios receives is what you choose to email us, for example through the game’s Send feedback or Report a problem form. When you ask, we delete all of it:

DataDeleted
Your email addressYes
Your messages to usYes
Game logs you attached (a run’s rolls, scores and in-game choices, and the game version)Yes

What we keep, and for how long

We keep nothing after a deletion request. Copies in our email provider’s backups are removed on that provider’s schedule, within 90 days. If you never ask, we keep feedback emails only as long as needed to reply and to fix the problem reported, and no longer than 2 years.

Data on your phone

Your saved run, statistics, collection and your last 3 runs’ game logs are stored only on your device and never sent to us. To delete them, uninstall Loaded Dice or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Loaded Dice → Storage → Clear storage). In the browser version, clear the site’s data.
