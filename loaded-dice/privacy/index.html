Loaded Dice: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jilly Bean Studios (“we,” “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with Loaded Dice (the “Game”), including the Android application and the version playable in a web browser. By installing or playing the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Game does not require or offer an account or sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies. It does not transmit information about you, your device or your gameplay to us or to any third party on its own.

The only information we receive is what you choose to send us by email, for example through the Game’s Send feedback or Report a problem form (Section 4): your email address, your message and the Game’s version, and, only if you choose to attach it, the game log of one run (its rolls, scores and in-game choices). We use this information only to respond to you, to investigate the problem you report and to improve the Game. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes, and you may ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the address in Section 9.

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share this information with third parties.

Deleting Your Data

To have Jilly Bean Studios delete the information you have sent us, email support@jillybeanstudios.com from the address you wrote from. The steps, what is deleted and how long anything is kept are on our Delete Your Data page.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the Game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • the run in progress, so that it may be resumed;
  • a game log of your most recent runs (at most three, counting the run in progress): the rolls, scores and in-game choices made, so that you may attach one to feedback if you wish;
  • gameplay statistics (such as runs played, wins and best scores) and a record of in-game items discovered; and
  • your Game settings (such as animation, sound, vibration and display preferences).

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. You may delete it at any time by uninstalling the application or by clearing the site data in your web browser. If you use a device backup service, such as Android backup to your Google account, that service may include this information in its backups, subject to its provider’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing, Ratings and Feedback Initiated by You

The Game allows you to share a summary of a daily run. If you choose to do so, the Game provides a short line of text (the date, the number of rounds cleared and the points scored) to your device’s share function or clipboard. You determine whether and where that text is sent. The Game sends nothing on your behalf.

The Game’s settings also let you share a link to the Game, rate it and send us feedback. Sharing passes a short line of text and the Game’s store link to your device’s share function. Rating opens the Game’s page in Google Play. Sending feedback, or reporting a problem from the end of a run, shows a short form in the Game. Closing the form sends nothing. Choosing Send opens your email application with a message addressed to us containing what you wrote and the Game’s version and, only if you tick the box to attach it, the game log of a run you choose (its rolls, scores and in-game choices, and the Game’s version; no personal information and no information about your device). Nothing is sent unless you send that email from your email application, where you may change or remove anything first. If you do send it, we use it only to respond to you and to improve the Game. From time to time, after a run, the Android application may ask Google Play to show its review prompt; Google decides whether it is shown, and any rating or review you give is handled by Google as described below. In each case the Game itself transmits no information.

5. Third-Party Services

Google Play. If you obtain the Game from Google Play, your download and installation are handled by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. Google provides us, through the Google Play Console, with aggregated statistics (such as the number of installations and uninstallations and the countries, device models and Android versions in use) and with crash reports that include technical details, such as the device model and Android version, but do not identify you. Ratings and reviews you submit on Google Play are public and are displayed under the name associated with your Google account.

Web hosting. The browser version of the Game is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes in accordance with GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not receive or have access to those logs.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

6. Device Permissions

The Android application uses your device’s vibration function to provide haptic feedback, which you may disable in the Game’s settings. The Game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or accounts.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we introduce any feature that collects information, such as advertising or cloud saves, we will update this Policy before that feature becomes available, and we will revise the effective date above. Your continued use of the Game after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Policy, please contact us at support@jillybeanstudios.com.
